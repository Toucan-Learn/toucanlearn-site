The steps to success

A starting point, useful practice and a longer-term direction. We keep adjusting the plan around the learner.

  1. Start with a free session

    Your free first session is a live diagnostic: we get to know you and how best to support you.

  2. Agree the next steps

    We use the diagnostic report to choose the skills to practise and a pace that suits the learner.

  3. Work towards your goal

    Sessions can combine focused skill-building, exam preparation and meaningful longer-term projects.

  4. Review the plan each week

    Your tutor writes a weekly lesson report, and our founder reads it. We review what is working and adjust the learner’s plan.

Inside a lesson

Our lessons

A familiar shape, adapted to you

Four simple stages, with activities and pace shaped around the learner.

  1. Hello and the plan

    A friendly start, a short plan and a choice of activities.

  2. Catch-up

    Talk about the week. Typing the answers can double as writing practice.

  3. Skills built into their interests

    Use games, websites or newspapers to practise a skill with guidance.

  4. Review and refine

    Look back at the work and choose what to practise next.

Example lesson outline · illustrative

Writing through a game

For a younger primary learner practising English.

Goal
Practise writing clear sentences.
What we do in the session
Type a catch-up, then write instructions for a game they enjoy.
Skill practised
Sentence building, spelling and putting ideas in order.
Next step
Read the instructions together and choose one sentence to refine.

Example lesson outline · illustrative

Working through an exam question

For an older student preparing for GCSE or IB exams.

Goal
Practise an answer that addresses the question.
What we do in the session
Use a worked example, try a past-paper question and review the mark scheme.
Skill practised
Planning an answer and applying exam technique.
Next step
Refine the answer and choose a question for further practice.

Example lesson outline · illustrative

English for a work qualification

For an adult working towards Functional Skills English for work.

Goal
Practise clear, practical writing for work.
What we do in the session
Read a workplace message, use a worked example and draft a reply.
Skill practised
Reading for meaning and choosing clear words and sentences.
Next step
Review the reply and choose a Functional Skills practice task.

Weekly reports

Your tutor writes you a report every week, in their own words, and our founder reads every one.

Each report covers:

  • What we worked on What we did, and why it matters for the learner. Example: "We used the maths behind a drinks can, its circumference and height, to design a label that fits it."
  • What stood out A specific moment of progress, so it gets noticed. Example: "She beat me at our word game for the first time, a sign her spelling and word recognition are getting quicker."
  • What we're still building The skills we'll keep practising over the coming weeks. Example: "Turning what he understands into answers that match the exact exam question, using past papers and mark schemes."
  • The plan for next session What comes next, so nothing is a surprise. Example: "Drafting and publishing a short review of a film she loved on her own website, then editing it together."

You always know what's happening and why, and we adjust the plan each week.
