The steps

  1. 01

    Learn about you

  2. 02

  3. 03

Where we teach

Online 1-1 sessions

Most of our lessons happen online, so we can work with learners anywhere in the UK and match you with the tutor who suits you best, not just the one who lives nearest.

How it works online

We meet on Google Meet, and each lesson runs on a shared set of Google Slides. The plan, the activities and the learner's own work all sit in one place, ready to build on next week.

What you need

A laptop or tablet with a camera, headphones if you have them, and a quiet spot. We send you the link before each session.

Where we teach

Inside a lesson

What a session looks like

Every session follows the same simple shape, built around the learner. We notice how they think, meet them where they are, support them, connect it back to the skill, and finish with a clear next step.

  1. Hello and the plan A friendly start and a short "today we will" list, often with a choice of activities.
  2. Catch-up A few quick questions about their week. Younger learners often type the answers, which is writing practice that doesn't feel like it.
  3. Into their interests Activities built around what they love, like games, robots, football or films.
  4. The skill A focused task that turns the activity into real learning, from sentence building to past papers, with breaks when they need them.
  5. A clear finish A final challenge, a quick look back at what went well, and what's coming next time.
Built around you

Different ways of working

There's no one right way to learn, so we choose from a few approaches depending on the learner, and most people end up with a mix.

Structured and straightforward

Clear explanations, worked examples and plenty of practice, for learners who like to know exactly what they're doing and why.

Creative and project-led

Learning by making things, like a website, a game or a newspaper, so the skills get used for something real.

The right scaffolding

Lots of help at first, then we step back bit by bit as you get more confident.

Learner-led

You choose where to go next and work things out for yourself, with us guiding rather than lecturing.

Exam preparation

Past papers, mark schemes and exam technique, with a clear plan right up to the exam.

SEND-informed

We adapt the pace, the way we explain things and the setting to suit the learner, build on what they're good at, and work closely with families.

Weekly reports

Keeping you in the loop

We send weekly lesson reports so you're always up to date, and our team works together to bring out the best in every session.

Every week your tutor writes you a report in their own words, and our founder reads every one. Each report covers:

  • What we worked on What we did, and why it matters for the learner. Example: "We used the maths behind a drinks can, its circumference and height, to design a label that fits it."
  • What stood out A specific moment of progress, so it gets noticed. Example: "She beat me at our word game for the first time, a sign her spelling and word recognition are getting quicker."
  • What we're still building The skills we'll keep practising over the coming weeks. Example: "Turning what he understands into answers that match the exact exam question, using past papers and mark schemes."
  • The plan for next session What comes next, so nothing is a surprise. Example: "Drafting and publishing a short review of a film she loved on her own website, then editing it together."

You always know what's happening and why, the learner gets their progress noticed, and our team can see what's working and adjust the plan each week.
