Our approach

Every learner gets their own plan.

We start by getting to know you, then pick the way of working that suits you best, and we keep adjusting it as we go.

The steps

How it works

  1. 01

    Learn about you

    Your free first session is all about you, how you learn, what you're into and where the gaps are, and that shapes everything we do after.

  2. 02

    Immediate skill-building

    Straight away we work on specific skills in small, manageable steps, so progress is easy to see and confidence builds.

  3. 03

    Long-term projects and goals

    Alongside that we work towards something bigger, either a project you actually care about, like your own website, a game or a newspaper, or a goal like getting ready for your exams, so the skills have a reason to exist.

Built around you

Different ways of working

There's no one right way to learn, so we choose from a few approaches depending on the learner, and most people end up with a mix.

Structured and straightforward

Clear explanations, worked examples and plenty of practice, for learners who like to know exactly what they're doing and why.

Creative and project-led

Learning by making things, like a website, a game or a newspaper, so the skills get used for something real.

Vygotsky-inspired

We work just beyond what you can do on your own, with lots of support at first, then step back bit by bit as you get more confident.

Montessori-inspired

Hands-on and learner-led, with you choosing where to go next and working things out for yourself, and us guiding rather than lecturing.

Exam preparation

Past papers, mark schemes and exam technique, with a clear plan right up to the exam.

SEND-informed

We adapt the pace, the way we explain things and the setting to suit the learner, build on what they're good at, and work closely with families.
