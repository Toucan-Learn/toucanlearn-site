An easy sign-up process.

Tell us the broad goal and current difficulty. We will take it from there.

  1. Start with a free session

    We use the first free-trial as a live-diagnostic session to get to know you and the best ways we can support you.

  2. Agree the next steps

    We use the diagnostic report to choose the skills to practise and a pace that suits the learner.

  3. Work towards your goal

    Sessions can combine focused skill-building, exam preparation and meaningful longer-term projects.

  4. Review the plan each week

    Your tutor writes a weekly lesson report, and our founder reads it. We review what is working and adjust the learner’s plan.
