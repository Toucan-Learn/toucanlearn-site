Getting started Projects & learning Exams & qualifications

An easy sign-up process.

Tell us the broad goal and current difficulty. We will take it from there.

  1. Start with a free session

    Your free first session is a live diagnostic: we get to know you and how best to support you.

  2. Agree the next steps

    We use the diagnostic report to choose the skills to practise and a pace that suits the learner.

  3. Work towards your goal

    Sessions can combine focused skill-building, exam preparation and meaningful longer-term projects.

  4. Review the plan each week

    Your tutor writes a weekly lesson report, and our founder reads it. We review what is working and adjust the learner’s plan.

GCSE English and maths

Sports journalism

The spark

Match predictions brought English and maths together: make a case and use statistics to support it.

What we built

A newspaper of previews, opinion and analysis, including a case against hydration breaks and “Leeds: a case for 13th”.

Skills

Persuasive writing, evidence and data, structure and editing.

English · game design

DARTY

The spark

A football game directed by the learner. Every change starts with a clear written request.

What we built

Those requests become real updates, from player movement to goalkeeper reactions. A short video showcase is planned.

Skills

Precise instructions, sequencing, cause and effect, and revising.

English · writing and design

Drink design

The spark

Drink ideas became labels and adverts. Pencil drawings became 3D renders.

On the label

Fizzy: “light, bright red” and “Fizzy, extreme.” Ground Pinch, from a football phrase: “designed for everyone”. Skids: “When you're in a rush, have some Skids.”

Skills

Audience, word choice, persuasive language and design thinking.

Music · notes and patterns

Piano

The spark

A way into music through play: hear a note, spot a pattern and try again.

Try it

Play the orange note, then the next green note. The tempo bar helps you keep time. Try a key or your computer keyboard.

Skills

Listening, recognising notes and patterns, and keeping time.
