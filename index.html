Your team

A team behind every learner, not just a tutor.

We're not a tutor marketplace, so nobody gets left on their own. Your tutor works with our founder (qualified SEN & education specialist) and the wider Toucan team. Our tutors are trained and write weekly reports, and we review and refine every learner's plan each week.

Our approach

Every learner gets their own unique plan.

  1. 01

    Learn about you

    We use the first free-trial as a live-diagnostic session to get to know you and the best ways we can support you.

  2. 02

    Develop your skills

    Our way is to start planning effective and tailored skill-building tasks at a calibrated selected pace to your needs.

  3. 03

    Long-term goals and projects

    At Toucan Learn we like to use short-term skill building exercises alongside long-term objectives, like exam preparation and meaningful personal or commercial projects.

We send weekly lesson reports so you're always up to date, and our team works together to bring out the best in every session.
