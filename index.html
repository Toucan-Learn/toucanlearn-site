How it works

A clear process, without forcing every learner down the same route.

Learners and families should know exactly what happens next. The plan stays responsive because every session gives us new evidence about what helps the learner engage, understand and progress.

Understand→ Engage→ Build→ Review

A consistent method. A different route for every learner.

  1. 01

    Tell us the goal

    Reveal the detail+

    Email us who the support is for, the subject or goal and your rough availability. We will check the tutor fit and arrange your free first session.

  2. 02

    Try your free first session

    Reveal the detail+

    Your first session is free. Meet Joshua or another Toucan tutor, explore your starting point and agree a useful way forward.

  3. 03

    Tailored weekly sessions

    Reveal the detail+

    If you continue, sessions are £40 per hour. Each has clear learning aims, with the structure, pace and interests that work for you.

  4. 04

    Review and next step

    Reveal the detail+

    Your tutor records what was covered, what helped and what needs more work. Joshua reviews progress with the tutor and helps plan the next useful step.
