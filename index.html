Evidence standard

Real proof deserves permission and context.

Toucan does not publish a learner story, result, quotation or work sample simply because it sounds impressive. The exact material must be checked, anonymised where appropriate and approved for public use first.

What proper evidence includes

Enough detail to understand the progress—without exposing the learner.

When a case study is approved, it should explain the genuine starting point, the support delivered, the timeframe, the outcome and any important limits.

The learner does the work. Toucan should describe its contribution accurately and never imply that one outcome is a promise to somebody else.

Starting point The goal and difficulty

Useful context, stripped of unnecessary identifying details.

Support What was actually provided

The teaching route, scope and timeframe—not a vague claim.

Outcome What changed, with limits

Evidence presented honestly, without guarantees or inflated attribution.

No learner material appears here until its exact public use has been agreed.
