How it works

Get to know them, build the skills, make it matter.

Every learner starts the same way, with us getting to know them properly, what they enjoy, what they find hard and what they'd like to get out of it.

  1. 01

    Learn about you

    Your free first session is all about you, how you learn, what you're into and where the gaps are, and that shapes everything we do after.

  2. 02

    Immediate skill-building

    Straight away we work on specific skills in small, manageable steps, so progress is easy to see and confidence builds.

  3. 03

    Long-term projects and goals

    Alongside that we work towards something bigger, either a project you actually care about, like your own website, a game or a newspaper, or a goal like getting ready for your exams, so the skills have a reason to exist.

You'll get a short report after every lesson, and Joshua oversees every learner's plan.
