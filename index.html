Our approach

Every learner gets their own unique plan.

  1. 01

    Learn about you

    We use the first free trial as a learning assessment to get to know you and the best ways we can support you.

  2. 02

    Develop your skills

    Our way is to start planning effective and tailored skill-building tasks at a calibrated selected pace to your needs.

  3. 03

    Long-term goals and projects

    At Toucan Learn we like to use short-term skill building exercises, and also focus on long-term objectives, like exam preparation or meaningful long term projects.

We send weekly lesson reports so you're always up to date, and our team works together to bring out the best in every session.
