# Toucan Learn: first four weeks of lead generation

25 October 2026. Start with **the warm pipeline, current-client referrals, then Google Business Profile and reviews**. Do not launch five channels at once. With five active clients, roughly ten warm names and less than two hours a week, personal follow-up is a more sensible first bet than building an audience from scratch.

## Source and assumptions

Source: Alex Hormozi, *$100M Leads*, Acquisition.com Volume II, 2023. The EPUB was found in Downloads and converted with Pandoc to `../outputs/growth-review-20261025/100M Leads.txt`. Relevant chapter titles are cited below. These are adaptations to this business, not the book's suggested volume targets.

The book distinguishes a contact from someone showing interest. Its useful short formulation is “Engaged leads are the true output of advertising.” For this plan, an **enquiry** means a new parent or adult learner who describes a relevant need and is willing to discuss tutoring. A like, an old name, a review, a school acknowledgement and a duplicate follow-up are not new enquiries.

Assume the ten warm names are previous enquirers or people who invited a relevant follow-up. Do not treat every personal contact as permission for marketing. Use the established conversation channel. Assume no more than one family per active client when estimating referral capacity. No named client information was fetched or exported for this plan, and no outreach messages were sent.

The targets below are planning hypotheses, not historic conversion rates or promises. Review after four weeks. The pool is small, so the ranking can change quickly.

## Ranked channels and monthly targets

Effort below covers the channel actions. Shared reply handling and free-session fulfilment are budgeted separately. The enquiry-per-hour estimates are explicit assumptions for month one, not measured data.

| Rank | Channel | Weekly channel time | Four-week target | Working enquiry/hour estimate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Warm pipeline | 15 minutes | Contact all 10 names once and send up to 6 single follow-ups; 2 engaged enquiries, 1-2 first sessions booked | About 2 enquiries per channel hour |
| 2 | Current-client referrals | 10 minutes | 4 individual asks, at most one per family; 1 new enquiry and 1 first session booked | About 1.5 enquiries per channel hour |
| 3 | Google Business Profile/reviews | 10 minutes | 2 neutral review invitations, aim for 1-2 genuine reviews, accurate profile and replies; 0-1 attributable enquiry | About 0-1.5 enquiries per channel hour initially |

Across channels, aim for **3-4 distinct enquiries, 2-3 first sessions booked and 1 suitable recurring client** in the first month. These are modest targets chosen for capacity, not a promise. Record the first source once so a referral that later uses Google is not counted twice. One recurring one-hour lesson a week is £40/week in revenue, before all delivery costs; do not confuse that with profit.

### 1. Warm pipeline: make the next step easy

**Weekly action:** Spend 15 minutes on the existing ten names. Initial contacts: three in week 1, three in week 2, two in week 3 and two in week 4. From week 2, use remaining time for up to two follow-ups to people contacted at least seven days earlier. Finish any remaining single follow-ups over weeks 5 and 6, within the same time cap. Stop after one unanswered follow-up, a decline or a request not to contact. If replies use the time, reduce new messages.

Personalise the first sentence from the existing conversation. Use the parent or adult page as appropriate.

> Hi [name], you mentioned [the subject or goal] when we last spoke. Are you still looking for some help with that? Toucan's first session and diagnostic report are free, and ongoing lessons are £40/hour with weekly reports. If it would help, reply with the learner's level and whether online would suit, and I'll suggest the next step. No need to send reports or medical details here. Joshua

One follow-up:

> Hi [name], just checking whether help with [goal] is still useful. If the timing has changed, that's fine and I won't keep chasing. Joshua

After an interested reply, offer two actual available times and name the proposed tutor. Do not invent availability, response deadlines or “last places”. Do not send the whole offer review as a message.

### 2. Current-client referrals: ask at a natural point

**Weekly action:** Spend up to ten minutes asking one current client or parent whether they know someone who could use the service, after normal service delivery and a real conversation. Four asks over the month is enough for the four recurring clients. Include the project client only when appropriate to their work and contact preferences. Do not ask every family every week or repeatedly link the request to a child's progress.

> Hi [name], if someone you know is looking for tutoring built around the learner, would you be comfortable forwarding the short note below? There is no need to share anyone's details with me. They can contact me themselves if it is useful. Joshua

Forwardable note:

> Toucan Learn offers SEND-informed tutoring for children and adults, online across the UK. The first session and diagnostic report are free; after that it is £40/hour with weekly lesson reports. You can ask Joshua about the subject, goal and tutor fit here: https://toucanlearn.co.uk/contact.html

A parent's personal recommendation must be their own words. Do not give them a fabricated story or ask them to repeat a result claim. No referral discount is needed for this first test.

### 3. Google Business Profile and reviews: maintain proof

**Weekly action:** Spend up to ten minutes checking for new reviews and replying briefly, correcting factual profile information if needed, and checking that the website/contact route is right. In weeks 1 and 3, invite one actual client who has not already reviewed. Rotate independently of whether feedback is positive; do not invite only people expected to leave five stars. Avoid asking the same person for a referral and a review in the same week.

Week 1's ten-minute slot prioritises accurate service description, contact information, hours and coverage. Use the real Bristol base and actual service areas; do not create fictional London/Henley locations. Reuse an existing permission-cleared project image only when it helps explain a service. A weekly photo or post quota is unnecessary.

> Hi [name], if you would like to share an honest account of your experience with Toucan Learn, a Google review can help other parents and adult learners decide whether to contact us. It is completely optional. Please leave out learner names and personal details. Here is the review link: [the existing Google review link]. Thank you, Joshua

Use the existing review URL from `index.html:270`, checked against the Business Profile before sending. The site currently has a leave-review link; showing visitors how to read real reviews is also needed. A short public reply can simply thank the reviewer for their feedback. Do not disclose the learner's SEND status, school, goals or work in the reply.

Google identifies relevance, distance and prominence as local ranking factors. Complete information and genuine reviews can help; this does not mean a post each week will produce rankings. See [Google's local ranking guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en). Do not pay, discount or give free lessons in exchange for reviews, and do not filter requests to solicit only positive reviews. See [Google's review guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122?hl=en) and [fake-engagement policy](https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114?hl=en).

## Time cap: 115 minutes, including acquisition fulfilment

| Weekly activity | Minutes |
| --- | --- |
| Warm messages/follow-ups | 15 |
| One referral request | 10 |
| Business Profile/review maintenance | 10 |
| Reply handling and arranging next steps, spread over the week | 15 |
| Update the three-number scoreboard | 5 |
| Reserved time for free first-session delivery and its report | 60 |
| **Total cap** | **115** |

The 60-minute reservation is a planning allowance, not a claim about the current session duration. If a free lesson plus report actually takes 75 minutes, reduce outreach/admin from 55 to 40 that week. In general, **outreach/admin time must not exceed 115 minus acquisition-delivery minutes**. If a booking would exceed that limit, offer the next available week. Do not shorten a promised lesson or report to fit the spreadsheet. Paid lesson delivery and its existing reports are ordinary service delivery, not additional lead-gen work.

When no free session is booked, leave the spare capacity unused or use a bounded school/group test below. Do not automatically fill it with more design work. Week 1's offer/content decisions also use this allowance; if they take time, start with fewer outreach actions.

## Schools and parent groups: the next test, not extra weekly obligations

After the ten warm names and their single follow-ups are covered, replace the 15-minute warm slot with one of these tests. Do not add both to the schedule. Use a real existing introduction first. Results may take longer than a month.

**SENCO/school test:** One personalised message a week for four weeks, using a public professional contact or a warm introduction. State what is actually offered and link the existing Children and safeguarding pages. Aim for one substantive reply and permission to share information with interested parents; target 0-1 parent enquiry in that first month. Do not count a school reply as a parent enquiry.

> Hello [name], I'm Joshua, founder of Toucan Learn. We offer SEND-informed one-to-one tutoring online across the UK, with a free first session and diagnostic report, then £40/hour with weekly lesson reports. If families at [school] ask about this kind of support, would it be useful for me to send a short outline of subjects, tutor matching and our safeguarding arrangements? Parents can contact us directly; I am not asking you to send pupil information. Our current overview is https://toucanlearn.co.uk/children.html. Best, Joshua

This is an adaptation of the partnerships chapter, not a paid affiliate arrangement or a claim of school endorsement. Keep school procurement work out of the initial plan.

**Local parent-group test:** In one group whose rules permit it, spend up to 15 minutes once a week answering a relevant request or sharing one existing anonymised project with permission. Do not cold-message members. Aim for two useful permitted contributions and one enquiry over a month, not a posting streak.

> I run Toucan Learn, so I should be clear about that connection. For [the issue raised], one manageable first step is [a specific suggestion relevant to the question]. If tutoring would help, we offer a free first session and diagnostic report, then £40/hour. Our Children page explains the approach: https://toucanlearn.co.uk/children.html. Happy to answer a general question here; please keep learner details private.

The bracketed suggestion must be useful and tailored before posting. An existing project story is evidence to explain a method, not permission to promise the same result to another child.

## The three-number weekly scoreboard

Use one line in the existing notes, with the week ending date:

**Invitations sent / new engaged enquiries / free first sessions booked**

Example format: `Week ending 1 Nov: 4 / 1 / 0`. That is an example, not a reported result. Count individual warm, referral and neutral review invitations as inputs; do not count repeated follow-ups as new people. Aim for about four invitations and one new enquiry in a normal week, and two to three bookings across four weeks. Capacity takes precedence over the input target.

In the same existing conversation note, keep the source and next action. At month end, check whether any first sessions became suitable recurring work. Do not add a CRM, dashboard or automated email sequence. After four weeks, compare enquiries with the capped channel time. If there are no enquiries, inspect the actual messages and offer before adding channels. If there are enquiries but no bookings, fix fit, availability and the next step. If trials do not continue, review the service match and delivery.

## Stop doing

- Adding visual features while the example testimonials remain public.
- Treating follower counts, profile views or names on a list as enquiries.
- Repeatedly chasing the same ten people or asking the same four parents every week.
- Writing generic daily tips, starting a podcast, or designing a new lead magnet.
- Offering free work without counting session, report and follow-up time.
- Adding paid ads, paid tools, agencies, referral software or school commissions.
- Promising grades, inventing urgency or asking for a scripted positive review.
- Sending people into the enquiry form before one real end-to-end delivery check has passed.

The first month is a small, measured use of existing relationships and proof. It is not a reason to build another system.
