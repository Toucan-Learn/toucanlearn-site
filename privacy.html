How Toucan Learn proposes to collect, use and protect personal information.

Prepared page — not yet published. The enquiry system, providers, address and retention periods must be confirmed before this notice becomes operative.

Who is responsible

Joshua Gordon Jones, a sole trader trading as Toucan Learn, is the controller responsible for deciding how personal information is used. The public correspondence address will appear here before enquiries open. The intended privacy contact is hello@toucanlearn.co.uk, once that mailbox is active and tested.

Information we may collect

At first enquiry, Toucan Learn asks only for the parent, carer or adult learner’s contact details; whether support is for a child or adult; an optional approximate age or school year; the requested support; a brief description of the goal; preferred format; broad location where in-person support is requested; availability; whether a private matching conversation is requested; and limited campaign-attribution values. The form does not ask for a child’s name or invite free-text access, SEND or medical details.

Please do not submit diagnostic reports, medical records, full school records or urgent safeguarding information through the first-enquiry form. If more sensitive information is genuinely needed, Toucan Learn will explain why and arrange an appropriate private route first.

If tutoring proceeds, limited billing details, lesson arrangements, learning goals, progress notes and parent or carer communications may also be needed. Safeguarding and complaint records are kept separately with restricted access.

What you need to provide

There is no legal requirement to make an enquiry. Fields marked as required are needed so Toucan Learn can understand and respond to it; without them, Toucan Learn may not be able to reply or assess suitable support. Fields marked optional may be left blank. If tutoring is agreed, some identity, contact, billing and scheduling information will be needed to set up and administer the service.

Where information comes from

Most initial information comes from the adult learner or from a parent or carer enquiring for a child. During an agreed service, limited information may also come from the learner, tutor, parent or carer, or another person the customer asks Toucan Learn to communicate with.

The proposed legitimate interests are administering and improving the agreed service, keeping accurate minimum records, protecting people and systems, and resolving questions or disputes. Toucan Learn must document why each use is necessary and balance it against the person’s rights before relying on this basis.

Health, disability and other special-category information requires an Article 6 basis and a separate Article 9 condition. Toucan Learn will document the condition and necessity before collecting such information; a general form acknowledgement is not treated as special-category consent.

Who may receive information

Information is shared only where necessary with authorised Toucan Learn personnel, the approved form and email providers, a prospective tutor using minimum matching information, professional advisers, insurers, payment providers, or public authorities where necessary and lawful. Toucan Learn does not sell enquiry information or use it for unrelated marketing.

The final notice will name or clearly categorise the actual processors and describe any relevant transfer outside the UK before the form opens.

How long information is kept

The final notice will state the approved periods for unsuccessful enquiries, active and former client records, complaints, safeguarding records and campaign attribution. Financial records needed for Self Assessment are retained for at least five years after the 31 January submission deadline for the relevant tax year, and longer if another applicable HMRC rule, enquiry or dispute requires it. Non-financial learning information is not kept for that period merely because a client was invoiced.

Security and transfers

The launch standard is person-specific accounts, multi-factor authentication where available, minimum-necessary access and restricted client records. These controls, provider locations, contracts and any international-transfer safeguard must be verified before enquiries open.

No tuition connected with Russia is publicly offered while the exact sanctions, payment and international-transfer position remains unreviewed.

Children

A parent or carer should submit the initial enquiry for a child unless Toucan Learn later adopts a safe, age-appropriate direct-enquiry route. Information about a child is collected only where needed to consider or provide tutoring, keep them safe or meet another identified lawful purpose.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, people may have rights to access and correct their information, have it deleted, restrict its use, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent where consent is used. The final notice will explain which rights apply to each purpose and how to exercise them. Where Toucan Learn relies on consent, it can be withdrawn through hello@toucanlearn.co.uk once that mailbox is active; withdrawal will not affect processing that was lawful before it.

People may also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office. Toucan Learn asks people to raise a concern directly first where they are comfortable doing so.

Your right to object

You may object to use of your information based on legitimate interests. You may also object at any time to direct marketing. Toucan Learn must consider an objection and explain the outcome. The final notice will provide the tested privacy contact route.

Cookies and automated decisions

The current candidate website does not use advertising pixels, behavioural advertising or analytics cookies. The enquiry form does not make automated decisions or profile people. Hosting logs and any later tools will be reviewed and described before release.
