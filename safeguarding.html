Children and adults at risk

Safeguarding.

Who to contact and how we keep lessons safe.

If someone is in immediate danger

Call 999.

Our safeguarding lead

Joshua Gordon Jones, Toucan Learn's founder, is the safeguarding lead. To raise a concern, email hello@toucanlearn.co.uk, call 07309 639095 or WhatsApp 07307 045816.

Our tutors

Joshua recruits and oversees Toucan Learn's tutors. Joshua Gordon Jones, Joan Miquel Serra and Connor Wright hold enhanced DBS certificates.

Joshua is Team Teach qualified in positive behaviour support.

Online safety

For under-18s, online lessons should take place in a shared space at home. Tutors do not have private social media contact with learners.

Other people you can contact

Adults concerned about a child can contact the NSPCC Helpline. Children and young people can contact Childline.
