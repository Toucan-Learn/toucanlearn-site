How it works

  1. Start with a free session

    Your free first session is a live diagnostic: we get to know you and how best to support you.

  2. Agree the next steps

    We use the diagnostic report to choose the skills to practise and a pace that suits the learner.

  3. Review the plan each week

    Your tutor writes a weekly lesson report, and our founder reads it. We review what is working and adjust the learner’s plan.

SEND support

Enjoy the learning

Start with their interests, so the activity matters in its own right.

A setting that works

Adapt the space and pace, with breaks and no pressure early on.

Notice the way in

A joke, an interest or a pause often shows us how they learn best.

Their ideas, their words

We help shape the thought; the opinion and the work stay theirs.

The next step, together

Work just beyond what they can do alone, then gradually step back.

Long-term goals and projects

Review progress towards goals, develop skills through hands-on, real-world projects.
