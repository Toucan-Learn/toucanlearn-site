School, qualifications and everyday communication

English

Build reading, writing, speaking and listening around what the learner needs to do.

Primary and GCSE

Practise spelling, sentence building, reading and longer writing. At GCSE, work with the course, questions and mark schemes to plan relevant answers.

Aaron teaches English up to GCSE. Joshua also supports English; we agree the learner's level and tutor fit at enquiry.

Adults and Functional Skills

Support with Functional Skills English, work communication and everyday literacy. A message, form, job advert, application, workplace document or conversation can become the starting point for useful practice.

Practise clear questions and instructions, understand job adverts, express relevant experience and prepare for interviews.

Clear boundary: Toucan provides tutoring and language practice. We do not guarantee a job, visa, immigration result, qualification or particular language level.

Primary through GCSE, and adult numeracy

Maths

Calm one-to-one support for school maths through GCSE Higher, working life and everyday confidence with numbers.

School and GCSE maths

Build missing concepts, subject language and usable methods around the learner’s current curriculum. GCSE Foundation and Higher support starts with the learner's understanding, topics, exam board and exam date.

Aaron teaches maths up to GCSE, and Joshua supports maths. The level, goal, deadline and tutor competence are checked before a route is agreed.

Everyday numeracy

Practise numbers for work and daily life through relevant calculations, forms, time, money or measures.

We look beneath the error to see whether the issue is a missing concept, language, working memory, anxiety, pace or explanation.

Clear boundary: We do not guarantee grades or qualifications. Complex needs and exceptions receive Joshua’s review before tutoring is agreed.
