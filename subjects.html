IB and A-level with Joshua

Psychology

Clear explanations, exam technique and coursework support with our founder, a psychology graduate.

Understand and explain

Work through course topics, connect studies to the question and practise short and extended answers. Use past papers and mark schemes to develop a clear plan for exam preparation.

Planning and feedback

Support with planning and improving the IB Internal Assessment (IA) and Extended Essay. Bring the course requirements, current draft or question so we can agree useful next steps.

Tell us whether you are studying IB or A-level Psychology, the topic or paper, and when the exam or coursework deadline is.
