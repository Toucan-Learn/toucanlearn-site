Your team

A team behind every learner, not just a tutor.

We're not a tutor marketplace, so nobody gets left on their own. Your tutor works with our founder and specialist and the wider Toucan team, our tutors are trained, and we review and refine every learner's plan each week.

Joshua Gordon Jones

Founder · Psychology graduate & SEN specialist

Joshua has a 2:1 in Psychology, with a first-class mark (80) in neurodevelopmental disorders. He's worked with children and in education for over seven years, first as a lead football coach and now as a tutor, and he's been teaching learners with ADHD, autism, dyslexia and DLD since day one. He also has over five years of professional writing experience.
