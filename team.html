A team behind every learner, not just a tutor.

We're not a tutor marketplace, so nobody gets left on their own. Your tutor works with our founder and specialist and the wider Toucan team, our tutors are trained and check in after every session, and we review and refine every learner's plan each week.

We teach online, with in-person sessions in Bristol, London and Henley, subject to tutor availability. Each tutor's subjects and local availability are set out below.

Joshua Gordon Jones

Founder · Psychology graduate and SEN specialist

“Hallo, my name is Josh. I started this because too many students are forgotten about, or handed worksheets and left to get on with it. We started Toucan so every learner has a team behind them.”

I have a 2:1 in BSc Psychology, with a first-class mark (80) in neurodevelopmental disorders. I’ve worked in education for over seven years, first as a lead football coach, then as a one-to-one SEN specialist, and now running Toucan Learn. I also have over five years of professional writing experience and am currently preparing for a PhD in Educational Psychology. I’ve been teaching learners with ADHD, autism, dyslexia, PDA and DLD since day one. I oversee every lesson and work closely with our team to provide the most effective support possible.

Aaron is a star tutor, creating strong dynamics with students and producing fantastic results. Creative, sharp and hard-working, and you can see it in every lesson he plans. Aaron has SEN experience and is a great pick if you need a creative, fun and hardworking online tutor.
