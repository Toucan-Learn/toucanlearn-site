Terms.

The basics of learning with Toucan Learn.

Your first session

Your first session is free and includes a free diagnostic report.

Prices

Lesson prices are shown on our enquiry page.

Paying

Lessons are paid for weekly, in advance.

Moving or cancelling a lesson

Give us 24 hours' notice and you can move or cancel a lesson for free. With less notice we may charge for it, though we'll always try to rearrange.

Stopping

There is no contract. You can stop lessons at any time.

Safeguarding

Read our safeguarding page for our arrangements and how to raise a concern.
