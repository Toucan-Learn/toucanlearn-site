02 / Published writing

The Orbit Cup

By Kit · fictional match report

COMETS3 : 2MOON ROVERS

The Comets were one goal down with two minutes left. I thought the Orbit Cup was gone. Then our captain spotted a gap and passed instead of shooting. That mattered because three defenders were blocking the goal. We scored, and suddenly everybody was shouting.

The winner came from a corner. The ball bounced off the post and rolled to me. I hit it with my left foot, which is usually my terrible foot. This time it worked! We won 3–2. My favourite part was the pass before the equaliser. Being brave doesn't always mean taking the shot yourself.

How it grew +

First draft

We played the Moon Rovers. We were losing but then we scored two goals. I scored the last one. It was good and we won the cup.

Improved version

“The Comets were one goal down with two minutes left. I thought the Orbit Cup was gone.”

The full report above now explains why the pass mattered and ends with Kit's own reflection.

Sample tutor note: Kit kept their own voice, added a stronger opening and explained a reason, so the reader can picture the match.
